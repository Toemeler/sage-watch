## Sage Data (data-lab test build)

Only data, no face or other design. The watch's probe records every sensor and Health type the watch has and sends it to the iPhone. The iPhone shows it live and keeps every run.

**Install:** add `Sage.ipa` in SideStore, then install the watch app from the Watch app on iPhone (My Watch › Available Apps). This replaces Sage; install a `main` build to get the face back.

**Use:** on the iPhone, tap *Open on the watch and start*. You can also switch *Probe* on on the watch. Allow Health, Motion and Location when asked.
