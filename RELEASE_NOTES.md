## Install

**With SideStore (recommended, required for the watch app):** add `Sage.ipa` in SideStore. iOS then offers the watch app in the Watch app on your iPhone (My Watch → Available Apps → Sage → Install). Uses 2 of the 10 weekly App IDs of a free Apple ID; refresh in SideStore every 7 days.

**Inside LiveContainer:** the iPhone app runs (Today ring, Sleep, Plan, Faces preview). The watch app cannot be delivered from LiveContainer, and Health data is usually unavailable there, so steps and heart rate show a dash.

## In this build

- Watch: the Sage face full-screen, ported 1:1 from the prototype: railway track, applied indices, minute numerals, date window, Playfair time (ivory hours, italic sage minutes), steps and heart subdials, quick-action plaque. Modes switch on the biphasic plan (morning, lunch, afternoon, nap, after-nap, evening, sleeping, weekend). Always-on drops to the time, track and indices.
- iPhone: Today (24-hour ring with both sleep blocks and smart-wake windows), Sleep (night and nap hypnograms, stage totals), Plan, Faces (live face with a preview of every mode).
- Health: steps and latest heart rate, read only. Nothing leaves your devices.
